Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information SecureHub IT Solutions ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need, we do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising trackers.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us through the form

  • Your name, email address, and message.
  • Optionally: phone number, company name, and the service you are interested in.
  • Technical details used to prevent spam and abuse: a one-way (hashed) version of your IP address and your browser's user-agent text. We do not store your raw IP address in our database.

Anonymous website analytics (only if you click "Accept")

  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser (it does not contain your name or email).
  • Which sections of the page you viewed and how long you were active on the site.
  • The website you came from and campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source).
  • Device type, browser, operating system, screen size and language.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address.

If you click "Decline" (or ignore the banner), none of the analytics above is collected and no analytics ID is stored in your browser.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss our services with you.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam or misuse.
  • With your consent, to understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

3. Your choice about analytics

Analytics runs only after you accept it, and you can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our website, or here: . Resetting also removes the analytics IDs we stored in your browser.

4. Data stored in your browser

NameTypePurpose
securehub_consent Local storage Remembers your analytics choice (necessary).
securehub_visitor_id Local storage Random anonymous ID so we can tell returning visitors apart (analytics, only with consent).
securehub_session_id, securehub_visit_sent, securehub_active_ms Session storage Group one browsing session and measure active time (analytics, only with consent; cleared when you close the tab).

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We use hosting, database and email providers to run the website and handle enquiries; they process data only on our behalf. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs (for example, IP addresses) for security. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a record of our business communication. Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for understanding site performance. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted database access, hashing of IP addresses, and rate limiting to stop abuse. No online service can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To exercise any of these, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Our website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be highlighted on our website.
